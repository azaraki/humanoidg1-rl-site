What it does

Everything you need
to make a humanoid walk.

From raw physics simulation to policy export. One stack, every stage.

43-DOF Humanoid

Full Unitree G1 model: 29-DOF body plus two Dex3 three-finger hands. Ready to train, ready to deploy.

MuJoCo 3.8 Physics

High-fidelity contact dynamics. Deterministic rollouts, GPU-accelerated batched sim for 4096+ parallel envs.

PPO + Motion Tracking

Proximal Policy Optimisation out of the box. SONIC-style universal motion tracker with FSQ-tokenized rewards.

Interactive Sandbox

Tkinter sliders for every joint. Nudge the policy, perturb the base, inject disturbances live. No restart needed.

Motion Imitation

Imitate AIST++ dance and BONES-SEED retargeted clips. DeepMimic rewards, SMPL body model integrated.

ONNX Export

One command exports your policy to ONNX. Drop straight into C++/TensorRT for real-robot deployment.

How it works

From pixels to joints.

Four stages. Every one scripted, every one reproducible.

01 · SIM

Load

Spin up the G1 model in MuJoCo. Randomised terrain, mass, friction, actuator noise.

02 · TRAIN

Learn

PPO on an RTX 4090. 4096 envs, 5000 iterations, ~10 hours wall-clock.

03 · TEST

Verify

Interactive sandbox with live sliders. Perturb, probe, record MP4.

04 · DEPLOY

Ship

Export to ONNX. C++ runtime talks to the real G1 over the Unitree SDK.
